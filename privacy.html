MomentDrop Publisher - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MomentDrop Publisher handles information when users connect social media accounts and use the application to publish content.

1. Information We Process

MomentDrop Publisher may process authorized social media account identifiers, authorization tokens, videos selected by users, captions, titles, hashtags, and publishing status information.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate connected accounts, prepare user-requested content, upload or publish content, and provide application functionality.

3. Social Media Authorization

Access to social media accounts is obtained through authorization mechanisms provided by the relevant platform. MomentDrop Publisher does not require users to provide their social media passwords directly to the application.

4. Video Content

Videos selected by users may be transmitted to services required to process or publish the content. Temporary copies may be created when technically necessary.

5. Third-Party Services

MomentDrop Publisher may interact with third-party services and APIs necessary for its operation.

6. Data Sharing

Personal information is not sold to advertisers or data brokers. Information is shared with third-party services only when necessary to provide functionality requested by the user or when required by law.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect authentication credentials and other information processed by the application.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's functionality. Users may request deletion of data associated with MomentDrop Publisher by contacting us.
