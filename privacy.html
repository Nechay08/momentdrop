Privacy Policy.

MomentDrop Publisher

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how MomentDrop Publisher handles information when users connect supported social media accounts and use the application to prepare and publish content.

2. Information we process

MomentDrop Publisher may process information required to provide its publishing functionality, including account identifiers, authorization credentials provided by supported platforms, user-selected video files and publishing metadata such as titles, descriptions and hashtags.

3. How information is used

Information is used only as necessary to authenticate connected services, prepare user-selected content and perform publishing actions requested by the user.

4. Third-party platforms

MomentDrop Publisher interacts with supported third-party platforms through their available APIs and authorization systems. Information processed by those platforms is also subject to their respective privacy policies.

5. User content

Users choose which video files and publishing information are processed. MomentDrop Publisher does not claim ownership of user content.

6. Data security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect authentication information and other data processed by the application. Users should also protect access to their computer and connected social media accounts.

7. Data retention and access removal

Information is retained only as needed for the application's functionality or as required by applicable services. Users may revoke third-party account access through the relevant platform's account or security settings.

8. Policy updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as MomentDrop Publisher and its supported integrations evolve. The current version will remain available on this page.
